Gifts · New

The present is ready. Now make them earn it.

A present handed over in a bag is gone in a second. Matryoshka turns the handing over into a painted doll that is opened figure by figure. The present itself, or where to find it, sits in the smallest, solid figure. Every figure around it holds a note, a photo or a voice and has its own lock: it opens on the birthday morning, after a riddle only they can answer, near the café where you met, with a word their grandmother knows, or under a warm palm. Make it on your own or pass it round friends and family: everyone paints one figure and doesn't see the others. It travels as one file in any messenger, and the riddles are real encryption. Free, no ads, no internet.

What's inside

Everything Matryoshka does.

The present in the smallest

The innermost figure is solid, as in a real matryoshka. It holds the present: a note saying where to look, a photo, a link to the tickets.

A lock on every figure

Time, riddle, place, someone else's word, gesture. You decide how each one opens, and in what order.

Riddles that hold

The answer is the key: without it the figure can't be opened, even by taking the file apart. Case, «ё» and punctuation don't matter.

At the café where you met

A place lock opens only within 50–300 m. Paste a map link, coordinates, or stand there and tap «I'm here». The phone checks it itself.

Ask your gran

Someone else knows the code word, and the quest pulls the family in. Or a gesture: cover it with a palm, shake it, turn the phone over.

Together

A shared matryoshka goes round as a file in a set order. Everyone paints one figure and sees the others only as silhouettes; the last one seals it.

A word back

Under every figure the recipient can leave a few words or a voice note and send them back as a file. They stand next to the matryoshka you gave.

Five paintings

Khokhloma, Gzhel, Polkhov-Maidan, Semyonov and Vyatka, all drawn in code. Black lacquer or limewood, as you like.

Build from source

JDK 17+ (Android Studio's bundled JBR works) and the Android SDK. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, DataStore, WorkManager; the dolls are drawn in code.

git clone https://github.com/bigdevwhale/matryoshka.git
cd matryoshka
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug
./gradlew assembleDebug -Pdemo              # app.matryoshka.demo: a made-up shelf
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest -Prender --tests '*RenderScreens*'   # every screen to PNG, EN, RU and light
Good to know

Questions people ask.

Does the recipient need the app?

Yes. A matryoshka is a file, and only the app knows how to open it figure by figure. It's free and installs in a minute.

Can someone peek inside before the day?

Not past a riddle or a code word: the answer is the key. Time, place and gesture are checked by the app, so someone determined could get round those.

How does a shared one work without a server?

The file goes from hand to hand in a messenger. Each giver adds a figure and passes it on; the app hides the others' figures, and the last giver seals it and sends it to the one it's for.
