Stillcraft / Apps / Hushwood

Meditation · New

Sit quietly, and the forest will come to you.

Shy animals come out when you're still. Hushwood turns that into a practice: put the phone down beside you and sit, breathe, stretch or write a page. The longer and quieter you sit, the bolder the forest gets; a movement sends everyone back into the trees. A forest clearing, a lake, a mountain meadow and the sea shore, painted in pixel art for every hour and season, fill with 38 animals and a few rare guests. Skip a day and nothing is lost: the animals just grow a little shy again.

What's inside

Everything Hushwood does.

Stillness brings them

The phone lies still and its accelerometer listens for jolts. Quiet builds minute by minute: tits and robins first, a fox after eight minutes, a deer after twelve, a bear after twenty. Pick the phone up and they run, but come back sooner.

Four places, 38 animals

A forest clearing; a lake with ducks, swans, a beaver and a moose; a mountain meadow with marmots, an ibex and a golden eagle; a shore with puffins, seals and a whale far out. The lake, the mountains and the sea open as your minutes add up.

Rare guests

A white deer at dawn after half an hour of quiet. A kingfisher for someone who breathes by the water seven days of ten. A skein of cranes at the equinox. A snow leopard on winter nights.

A guardian chooses you

After two weeks one animal steps out and stays: wren, owl, heron, crane, bear, fox, hedgehog or deer, read from when, how long and how you practise. It comes closer the more days you sit, and sometimes leaves a gift by the stump.

A portrait, pixel by pixel

Every sitting paints part of the guardian's portrait in the colour of its hour: rosy dawn, golden day, amber dusk, blue night. A finished portrait goes up on the shelf.

Breath and sound

Four rhythms (calm 4–6, even, box, 4–7–8) with motes of light that gather on the in-breath. Real field recordings of wind, birds, a stream, waves, crickets and a fire, switched on or off mid-sitting, and a tingsha bell to end.

Offerings and the journal

Leave seeds, berries, nuts, an apple or honey on the stump; whoever likes it comes sooner. The field journal keeps everyone you've met, a herbarium of one-line notes pressed as leaves, and the year as the rings of a cut stump.

The real hour and season

Each place follows the clock outside: dawn, day, dusk and night, with spring flowers, summer fireflies, autumn leaves and winter snow. Every animal keeps its own hours and seasons.

Quiet and private

A home-screen widget with the place as it is now and an optional daily reminder. Everything stays on the phone: no account, no ads, no internet.

Build from source

JDK 17+ (Android Studio's bundled JBR works) and the Android SDK. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore; the places, the animals, the portraits and the icon are painted in code on the phone.

git clone https://github.com/bigdevwhale/hushwood.git
cd hushwood
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest             # stillness, the guardian, the portrait
./gradlew assembleDebug                 # app.hushwood.dev, installs next to a release
./gradlew assembleDebug -Pdemo          # app.hushwood.demo with half a year of sittings
Good to know

Questions people ask.

Is it a meditation app or a game?

A practice tracker first. The forest only rewards stillness and coming back: there are no streaks to lose, no scores, and nothing to do while you sit. Breathing, yoga, a journal page or a mindful walk count too.

What if I practise away from the phone?

Write it down afterwards: a class, a walk, a page. Fewer animals come to these, but the place remembers, and they count towards the guardian and the rings.

What does it keep?

Your sittings, notes and meetings, on the phone only: no account, no ads, no analytics, no internet permission at all.
