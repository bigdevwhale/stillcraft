Lipovka Post · Steps

Steps from your watch.

Lipovka takes steps from Health Connect, the phone's shared health shelf. Health Connect counts nothing itself: it only holds what other apps put on it. So if you allowed everything and the steps are still the phone's, the watch's app almost always isn't putting its steps there yet. Four checks below, two minutes in all.

1. Choose Health Connect in Lipovka

  1. In Lipovka open Settings (the gear) and find Steps.
  2. Tap Health Connect and allow reading Steps.
  3. Under the buttons it should say Steps come from Health Connect. If it says Health Connect is missing, tap Install Health Connect.

2. Let the watch's app write to Health Connect

This is the step most often missed. Allowing Lipovka to read is half of it; the app of your watch or band must also be allowed to write. Menu names shift a little between versions; look for Health Connect in the app's settings.

  • Samsung Health: ⋮ or ≡ → Settings → Health Connect → allow Samsung Health to write Steps.
  • Mi Fitness (Xiaomi, Redmi): Profile → Health Connect (sometimes under Third-party apps) → turn it on and allow Steps.
  • Zepp Life, Zepp (Amazfit): Profile → Add accounts → Health Connect.
  • Google Fit: Profile → ⚙ → Sync Fit with Health Connect.
  • Huawei Health (Huawei, Honor): it doesn't share with Health Connect. The Health Sync app carries its steps over: pick Huawei Health as the source and Health Connect as the destination.
  • Other watches (Garmin, Fitbit, Polar, Withings and others): look for Health Connect in the app's settings or connected services.

Then open the watch's app once and let it sync with the watch: steps reach Health Connect only after the watch has handed them to its app.

3. Check the steps are there

  1. Open Health Connect. On Android 14 and newer it lives in the phone's Settings (type Health Connect in the search there, or look under Security and privacy → Privacy); on older Android it is an app of its own.
  2. Go to Data and access → Activity → Steps → See all entries.
  3. Today's entries should come from your watch's app. If only the phone is there, go back to step 2: Lipovka can't take what isn't on the shelf.

4. Put the watch first

When both the phone and the watch count the same walk, Health Connect takes each minute once, from the source highest on its list. If the phone stands first, the watch's extra steps are dropped.

  1. In the same Steps screen open Data sources and priority.
  2. Drag your watch's app to the top, above the phone.

What happens next

Open Lipovka: it reads Health Connect when it opens and from time to time in the background. Steps from Health Connect count from the day you switched to it. Today catches up to the watch's total at once; the days before stay as the phone counted them, because letters already carried aren't redone. If the watch syncs late, its steps still arrive for up to a week back, and steps once in the bag are never taken away.

Still too few?

  • Health Connect shows the watch's steps, but Lipovka doesn't: in Lipovka tap Phone and then Health Connect again, and check reading Steps is allowed in Health Connect → App permissions → Lipovka.
  • Steps come only when the watch's app is opened: the phone stops it in the background. Allow it to run without battery limits (long-press its icon → App info → Battery → Unrestricted).
  • Nothing helps: write to us in Telegram or open an issue, and say which phone, which watch and which app it uses.
