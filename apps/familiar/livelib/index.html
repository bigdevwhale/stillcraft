Familiar · Import

Bring your LiveLib shelf along.

LiveLib has no export of its own, so here is a short script that makes one. You run it on your LiveLib page, on a computer: it reads your "Read" shelf and saves it as a file Familiar can import. Nothing goes anywhere else.

  1. On a computer, open livelib.ru, go to your profile and open the Read shelf. The address looks like livelib.ru/users/me/books/read.
  2. Press F12 (on a Mac, ⌥⌘J) and open the Console tab.
  3. Copy the script below, paste it into the console and press Enter. If Chrome refuses to paste, type allow pasting, press Enter and paste again.
  4. The script goes through the shelf page by page, shows its progress in the corner and downloads livelib.csv.
  5. Send the file to your phone, and in Familiar open Settings → Import books and pick it.

What comes across

Title, author, cover, year, ISBN, pages (minutes for an audiobook), genres, the cycle and the book's number in it, the date you read it and your rating (out of ten on LiveLib, so 9 and 10 become five stars). Familiar turns LiveLib's genres into its own. For every book the script asks LiveLib once more, so a long shelf takes a few minutes. The file uses the same columns as a Goodreads export, and a Goodreads export (My Books → Import and export) imports the same way; Familiar finds its covers by itself. You need to be signed in to LiveLib.

The script is all on this page, nothing is loaded from elsewhere. There is also a Python version for a computer.
