Familiar · Import

Bring your shelves along.

Familiar takes the books you keep elsewhere: LiveLib, Goodreads, Yandex Books, Livelib Backup and Bookmory. Each ends in one file you pick in Familiar under Settings → Books from another app → Pick a file. LiveLib and Yandex Books have no export of their own, so for them there is a short script below that makes one; it runs on your own page and sends nothing anywhere else.

From LiveLib

  1. On a computer, open livelib.ru, go to your profile and open the Read shelf. The address looks like livelib.ru/users/me/books/read.
  2. Press F12 (on a Mac, ⌥⌘J) and open the Console tab.
  3. Copy the script below, paste it into the console and press Enter. If Chrome refuses to paste, type allow pasting, press Enter and paste again.
  4. The script goes through the shelf page by page, shows its progress in the corner and downloads livelib.csv.
  5. Send the file to your phone, and in Familiar open Settings → Books from another app → Pick a file.

From Goodreads

Goodreads makes the file itself, no script needed, and it works from a phone's browser too.

  1. Sign in to Goodreads and open My Books → Import and export.
  2. Press Export Library. In a minute a link Your export from … appears under the button (reload the page if it doesn't).
  3. Tap the link: goodreads_library_export.csv downloads.
  4. In Familiar open Settings → Books from another app → Pick a file and pick it. Goodreads gives no covers, so Familiar finds them in book catalogues; that takes a few minutes and carries on next time if you close the app.

From Yandex Books

Yandex Books has no export either, so this script makes one from your profile page. It takes finished books, the ones you are reading and the ones you want to read, with covers, genres, audiobooks and comics. Yandex Books shows nobody the dates, ratings or page counts, so those stay behind. You don't even need to sign in: an open profile is enough.

  1. On a computer, open books.yandex.ru, go to your profile and open Books. The address looks like books.yandex.ru/@login/books/all.
  2. Press F12 (on a Mac, ⌥⌘J), open the Console tab, paste the script below and press Enter.
  3. In a few seconds yandex-books-login.csv downloads. Send it to your phone and pick it in Familiar: Settings → Books from another app → Pick a file.

From Livelib Backup

If you have already saved your LiveLib shelf with Livelib Backup, pick its file in Familiar as it is: CSV, JSON or the one "for Goodreads" all work. Familiar learns the shelf (Read, Reading, Want to read, Didn't finish) from the file's name, so keep the name it was saved with; a file for Goodreads carries the shelf inside. "Didn't finish" goes to Paused, and a date known only to the month becomes its first day.

From Bookmory

  1. In Bookmory, make a backup in its settings. You get a bookmory_….bookmory file.
  2. Pick it in Familiar: Settings → Books from another app → Pick a file. Shelves, dates, ratings, pages, covers and audiobooks come across, and your Bookmory collections become your shelves.

What comes across

Title, author, cover, year, ISBN, pages (minutes for an audiobook), genres, the cycle and the book's number in it, the date you read it and your rating (out of ten on LiveLib, so 9 and 10 become five stars). Familiar turns LiveLib's genres into its own. For every book the script asks LiveLib once more, so a long shelf takes a few minutes. The file uses the same columns as a Goodreads export, and a Goodreads export (My Books → Import and export) imports the same way; Familiar finds its covers by itself. You need to be signed in to LiveLib.

The script is all on this page, nothing is loaded from elsewhere. There is also a Python version for a computer.
