Stillcraft / Apps / Tsukumo

Tsukumogami for any hobby · New

Light a candle. Wake the things you love.

In Japan they say that things used long and well grow a soul: tsukumogami. Tsukumo keeps that for your hobbies, any of them. Practise, note the minutes and what you did, and the things you really use for it slowly come alive in a house that grows with you over years. Missed days take nothing away. Free, no ads, no account, no internet.

What's inside

Everything Tsukumo does.

Any hobby

39 ready-made hobbies in nine families, from guitar and watercolour to sourdough, chess, climbing and bonsai, each with the things people really use for it. Or your own hobby with your own things.

A thing of your own

Type “the mug with a cat” and it gets one of 21 silhouettes, a colour and a nature of its own, by the word or by chance. Every spirit is drawn in code, frame by frame.

A session is a candle

After practising, note the minutes and what you did: repeat, try something new, fix, finish, learn, share. Each thing loves one verb and grows faster from it. A timer can count the session for you.

Waking up

A thing shivers, opens one eye, then both, grows little legs and steps off the shelf as a house spirit. The first ones wake after a few weeks.

Years of growing

Awake spirits become Keepers with a red scarf, Elders with white brows and patina, Sages in a straw hat and, after many years of one hobby, Kami with a halo. Hobbies earn titles by hours, up to a Living treasure at 3000.

A hundred candles

The candles in the alcove are the old Hyakumonogatari: every hundred is a party and sometimes a wonder. Twelve of them, about ten years of practice, end with a house that is a kami itself.

Friends and little ones

Spirits of two hobbies practised on the same day become friends, and after a while they have a little one: one's shape in the other's colour.

No guilt

Missed days take nothing away. After a long break the spirits just sleep under the kotatsu, and a paused hobby waits in the storeroom chest; bring it back a year later and they remember.

The real seasons

The house follows the date and the hour: blossom in spring, fireflies on summer nights, maple leaves in autumn, snow on the eaves; at night the paper lamp and the candles light the room.

The chronicle

Year by year: who woke, who rose in rank, which wonders came, your hours in every hobby and your notes.

Build from source

JDK 17+ (Android Studio's bundled JBR works) and the Android SDK. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore; the house, the garden in four seasons and every spirit are pixel art drawn in code.

git clone https://github.com/bigdevwhale/tsukumo.git
cd tsukumo
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug   # app.tsukumo.dev, next to a real install
./gradlew assembleDebug -Pdemo              # app.tsukumo.demo: a few years already lived
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest -Prender --tests '*RenderArt*'   # scene and sprite sheets
Good to know

Questions people ask.

My hobby isn't in the list.

Make your own: give it a name and a family, then add the things you really use. Each gets a silhouette and colour from its name, and you can pick others.

What if I stop for a month?

Nothing is lost. The spirits sleep under the kotatsu until the next candle, and the chronicle notes that you came back.

How long until a Kami?

Years. A favourite thing of a hobby you practise three or four times a week becomes a Keeper in its first year, a Sage in five or six, a Kami later still. The twelve wonders of the house take about ten.

Is anything sent anywhere?

No. The app has no internet permission. Everything stays on the phone.
