Stillcraft / Apps / Hearthspell

Cozy pixel chores · New

The master has gone travelling. The tower is yours.

The old master left his tower to you, his apprentice. The tower is your home: every floor is a room, every chore is a spell with its own rhythm. Cast it and the floor shines; forget it and harmless imps move in. Master a spell and its helper wakes up, a broom or a teapot that paces the floor. Mana buys decor and clothes, the master writes once a week with a souvenir in the envelope, and the shopping list becomes a quest for reagents that brew into potions. A cat moves in, every helper can be patted, and named imps fill a bestiary. Cozy pixel art, no streaks to lose, no ads, no account, no internet access.

What's inside

Everything Hearthspell does.

Floors are rooms

Eleven kinds of room, each a detailed pixel scene: the kitchen is the Alchemy Lab, the bedroom an Observatory under a domed window, the bathroom the Spring Baths, the hallway the Gate Hall, the balcony a Greenhouse. Rename, reorder and add floors.

Spells with their own rhythm

45 ready-made chores with sensible intervals, from the dishes every day to the oven once a moon, each with a small incantation, or your own. Freshness fades slowly instead of a checklist that resets every morning.

Imps, not punishment

An overdue spell brings a cheeky imp to its floor. It chews socks and giggles, nothing burns down, and one cast sweeps it out. A dusty floor just waits for you.

Helpers wake up

17 helpers, from the Broom and the Sponge to the Teapot and the Satchel. Cast a spell five times and its helper wakes and starts pacing its floor, then grows through four levels.

A cat, pats and named imps

Tap a helper and it hops, says something and hearts float up. A cat moves in, gets a name and sleeps wherever you've just tidied. Eighteen imps, from Pip the Sock-Chewer to a Hallowimp in a sheet, each leave with a parting word and go into the bestiary.

Stars, potions, gifts and Sunday tea

Every rank a spell climbs lights a star in twelve constellations. Market trips leave essences for week-long potions, helpers leave trinkets after a quiet night, and on Sunday they gather for tea to tell you about the week. Seasons dress the tower, and a home-screen widget shows its windows.

Mana, decor and the master's requests

Every cast earns mana for decor, from a cat to a fireplace, and for robes and hats. Each week the master asks for the three spells that waited longest, worth double until Sunday, and twelve letters tell the story of his travels.

A market of reagents

The shopping list sorted into shop sections with tower names, the section guessed from the name. It learns how often you buy things, suggests what's running low, and market mode is a big one-hand checklist.

Build from source

JDK 17+ (Android Studio's bundled JBR works) and the Android SDK. Python 3 with Pillow to redraw the pixel art.

git clone https://github.com/bigdevwhale/hearthspell.git
cd hearthspell
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest     # unit tests
./gradlew assembleDebug         # debug APK
./gradlew installDebug -Pdemo   # app.hearthspell.demo with a lived-in tower
python tools/art/export.py .    # redraw every sprite and scene
Good to know

Questions people ask.

What if I skip a few days?

Nothing breaks. There are no streaks to lose. Spells fade, imps move in, and the next cast sweeps them out. Back after five days or more, the master welcomes you home and your first spell is a Great Spring Cleaning worth double.

Can I add my own chores?

Yes. Any name, any interval from a day to a year, three levels of effort and the helper it wakes. You can pause a spell while you're away.

Is it for a family?

This version is one tower on one phone. A shared tower for the whole household is planned.

What if I change phones?

Settings → Backup saves the whole tower, every cast and the shopping list into one JSON file. Restore it on the new phone.
