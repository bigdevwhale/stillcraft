What I promise

Plainly and honestly.

  1. The apps stay free. No paywalls, no features locked behind a donation, on Android or on iPhone.
  2. Android as before. Updates and fixes for Android keep coming as usual; on iPhone, Lipovka goes first.
  3. Open accounts. The sums above are updated by hand; every purchase is shown in Telegram with a receipt and a photo.
  4. Early access. Everyone who chipped in gets the iPhone betas through TestFlight before the release.
  5. Your name in the village. If you'd like, write your name with the gift: the residents of Lipovka will thank you in a letter in the iPhone version.
  6. What comes next is yours to choose. Every app will come to iPhone. Fogwalk, Familiar and Farwalker are next in line, and those who supported vote on which ones follow. Anything beyond the goals goes to fixes, requested features, the servers and the bot, and time for new apps.
