Anyway

Memento mori, minus the skulls

We're all gonna die anyway. So go do the thing.

You don't have years. You have Saturdays, about 4,000 of them if you're lucky. Anyway shows you that number, then helps you stop spending them on things the Sun won't remember.

Download APK Explore Anyway No internet permission at all
  • NowHow many Saturdays you have left, a question of the day, and the big orange ANYWAY button for the thing you're scared to do.
  • Worry FilterWill it matter in 5 years? In 100? When the Sun becomes a red giant? If not, it shrinks, spins and floats away.
  • Before it's too lateThings you'd regret not doing. Each one counts how long it has waited and turns orange after a week.
  • You're still aliveOne notification a day, at a random moment between 10:00 and 21:00.

SwissZen

The mindfulness multi-tool

Open the knife. A minute is enough.

Most mindfulness apps want a commitment. SwissZen is a pocket knife: unfold the one tool that fits the moment, use it, fold it back.

Download APK Explore SwissZen MIT licensed
  • BreatheA sphere that grows on the inhale. Box 4‑4‑4‑4, Calm 4‑7‑8, Coherent 5‑5, for 2, 5 or 10 minutes.
  • Wim HofGuided power-breathing rounds in four levels, with breath-hold targets from 0:30 up to 3:00+.
  • BellA mindfulness bell that rings with the app closed: random, by interval or at set times, with quiet hours.
  • PracticesFive micro-practices of 1–5 minutes and five meditations of 5–20.
  • JournalOne line a day, and an Echo: something you wrote weeks ago, brought back.

FocusShield

Deep work for Windows

Block the noise. Keep yourself honest.

A focus timer built on friction. While a session runs, distracting sites stop loading, chosen apps close, and quitting early means waiting 10 seconds and typing a phrase. When it ends, everything unlocks and you get a real break.

Download for Windows Explore FocusShield Windows 10 / 11 · 100% local
  • Intention firstEvery session starts with what exactly you'll get done, and ends with a check-in: did you make it?
  • Real blockingSites, YouTube tabs (even through a VPN), apps and folders stay locked until the timer ends.
  • Breaks that restore4‑7‑8 breathing with an animated circle, the 20‑20‑20 eye rule, a stretch and a glass of water.
  • A sprout that growsAn optional mascot grows a leaf with each pomodoro and blooms on the long break.

Ties

A gentle nudge to keep in touch

Friendships rarely end in a fight. They fade quietly.

University, a new job, a move, and suddenly a year passes without a call. Tell Ties who matters and how often you'd like to talk. It keeps them in sight and nudges you when someone has waited too long.

Download APK Explore Ties No internet permission · MIT licensed
  • Your peopleFamily, friends, childhood friends, work and study. Add them by hand or from contacts, with a rhythm from every week to every six months.
  • A ring that cools downEach avatar's ring empties as a contact comes due: green, then amber, then coral.
  • One tap to reach outCall, or write via SMS, Telegram or WhatsApp. After a call it asks “Did you reach them?”, so logging takes one tap.
  • What to talk aboutA gentle opener on every card, and notes worth remembering for next time.
  • One calm reminderAt most once a day, and only when someone is due or has a birthday.

Unhook

Quit a habit, one day at a time

Quitting isn't one big decision. It's a small one, made again every day.

Unhook counts every clean minute and turns it into money saved and time won back, celebrates each milestone and helps you ride out a craving. A slip doesn't erase your progress. Everything paid quit trackers lock behind premium is free here, with no ads and no account.

Download APK Explore Unhook No internet permission · MIT licensed
  • A live counterDays, hours, minutes and seconds for any number of habits, with a ring that fills toward the next goal.
  • Goals that climb on their own1 day, 3 days, a week, a month, and on up to 5 years. The next rung is always the goal.
  • SOS for cravings“I have a craving” opens paced breathing next to your own reasons. When the wave passes, one tap logs the win.
  • Savings and rewardsMoney not spent and time won back, a projection for a year and five, and a wishlist you buy from the savings.
  • Ten trophiesShields from bronze to emerald, a calendar of clean days and a short note at every milestone.

Dayquest

A habit RPG in pixel art

Habits stick when something small and alive depends on them. Turn your days into a quest.

Your habits, dailies and to-dos power a pixel hero: finish things and it levels up, finds pets, furnishes a home and beats bosses; skip your dailies and it gets hurt. Like Habitica, with richer pixel art, kinder mornings and content built to last for years. Free, with no account: your hero moves to a new phone with a file or a code.

Download APK Explore Dayquest No internet permission · MIT licensed
  • A living pixel sceneYour hero, pet and boss under a sky that follows the real time of day and the real season: blossom, falling leaves, snow, stars.
  • Boss fightsEvery finished task hits the boss; every missed daily lets it hit back. Beaten bosses return as Elite, Ancient and Mythic.
  • 88 pets and gear you can seeHatch pets from eggs and potions, and try gear on your hero before you buy it.
  • Built for yearsSeasonal bosses and potions, an endless forge from level 20, a boss of the week and 47 achievements up to five years.
  • Kind mornings, exact undoTick off yesterday's dailies before the day closes, rest at the inn when you're ill, undo any tap.

Serene

Meditation, visualization, affirmations

Calm is a skill that grows from a few minutes a day. A quiet place for your mind.

Serene brings guided meditations, guided visualizations and affirmations into one calm app. Every session is read by a gentle voice recorded ahead of time and built into the app, so it all works offline. Breathing, real nature sounds, a vision board and a mood journal come with it. Free, with no ads, no premium and no account.

Download APK Explore Serene No internet permission · MIT licensed
  • Guided, with a calm voiceTen meditations and ten visualizations, from calm in 3 minutes and a body scan to a safe place and a starry sky. The narration is built in and works offline.
  • 240 affirmationsTwelve themes, an affirmation of the day, favourites, your own lines and a daily mix read aloud with room to repeat.
  • A vision boardAdd what you want with a photo and how it feels. Visualize my board turns it into your own guided session, in your own voice if you like.
  • Breathing and real soundsSix breathing patterns with a glowing orb, and 17 soundscapes, from rain and ocean to a fireplace, that never audibly loop.
  • A gentle journalMood check-ins, three good things, a two-week mood chart, a practice calendar and a wheel of balance.

How they're made

Peace of mind shouldn't be a subscription.

Nothing leaves your phone

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your worries, journal, people, habits and birthday stay on the device. Anyway, Ties, Unhook, Dayquest and Serene don't even ask for internet access.

Free, with the source open

Every app is on GitHub. Download the APK or EXE directly, read the code, build it yourself. No trial that turns into a bill.

Made to be closed

No feed and no infinite scroll. Open for a minute, get what you came for, put the phone down. That's the whole point.

Take one minute.
