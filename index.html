Calm and worry

Breathing, meditation, and a way through thoughts that won't let go.

SwissZenAndroidBreathing exercises, a mindfulness bell, short practices and a one-line journal. SereneAndroidGuided meditations, visualisations and affirmations, read aloud and working offline. KeeperAndroidFor anxious and intrusive thoughts: notice them, let them pass, check what came true. KiteAndroidCome back to the present during the day: a few soft tugs ask where your mind has wandered. SpintopAndroidLearn lucid dreaming: dream signs, reality checks by day, a ritual at night, and a top that keeps count. HushwoodAndroidA cosy pixel-art meditation tracker: sit still and shy animals come out. HeartwoodAndroidBefriend a real tree: every visit is a ring, and the friendship grows only when you go and stand beside it. LongwaveAndroidFall asleep faster: sleep sounds, and spoken programmes that quiet the mind, with a timer.

Focus and the phone

Fewer feeds, fewer distractions, and help giving something up.

FocusShieldWindowsA focus timer for Windows that blocks distracting sites and apps until the session ends. ObolAndroidLess scrolling: time in Reels, Shorts and TikTok has to be earned with useful apps. UnhookAndroidQuit a habit: counts the days, the money saved, and helps you ride out a craving. VigilAndroidGive something up for a while (sugar, the phone in the morning) and keep the vow. Side AAndroidLove music again: one album a week, a whole side at a time, on a turntable or a tape deck. RewindAndroidLove films again: your watchlist as a nineties video rental store, two tapes at a time, with due dates.

Habits, goals and the body

Routines, chores, goals, fasting, cold water and walks, in a way you'll keep up.

DayquestAndroidHabits and to-dos as a role-playing game: your pixel hero levels up when you do them. WaypointAndroidSet goals and reach them with 17 proven methods: OKR, WOOP, the 12-week year and more. PodiumAndroidTime everyday tasks and race your own best time, with splits and medals. HearthspellAndroidHousehold chores as a cosy pixel game: each room is a floor of a wizard's tower. EmberAndroidAn intermittent fasting timer with the body's stages along the way. TemperAndroidCold showers, contrast showers and the sauna: a timer, a plan and your progress. FogwalkAndroidGet out for walks: the map of your city is covered in fog until you walk through it. Lipovka PostAndroidA pedometer where your steps carry the village post: letters and stories of a small village that go on for years.

Learning and reading

Remember what you learn, read more, and keep trying new things.

ScriptorAndroidRemember what you study: write it in your own words, review it before you forget. SteleAndroidMemorise words, terms, dates and quotes with spaced repetition. Imports Anki decks. FamiliarAndroidA reading tracker with a pixel pet that grows on the pages you read. SillAndroidTry new things again: sow a seed from a catalogue of new things, water it each time you go back, and when it blooms it's yours.

Family and friends

Time with your children and each other, bedtime stories, family photos, keeping in touch.

LanternAndroidMake up bedtime stories with your child from randomly dealt heroes, places and troubles. CampfireAndroidA talk by the fire with the people close to you: sparks bring questions, and the word of the day stays in the ash. MarblesAndroidFor parents: spend more evenings with your kids, and see what the phone takes. TesseraAndroidThe same questions to a child, yourself or a grandparent, year after year, on film or in words. Night TrainAndroidA date night for couples: hundreds of questions to see each other from a new side. Party of TwoAndroidDates as quests: the two of you are a party of heroes, the phone is the game master. FridgeAndroidA fridge door for two: the shopping list, whose turn, notes and worries, shared between your two phones at home. TiesAndroidReminds you to call and write to the people who matter, before you drift apart. NotchAndroidA weekly photo of each child on a door frame, so you can watch them grow. SilverboxAndroidA family camera that works like film: 24 frames, prints developed the next day.

You and your life

Your time, your wins, who you want to be, letters to the future, moments worth keeping.

AnywayAndroidSee how many Saturdays you have left, and stop putting off what matters. CalaveraAndroidMake peace with growing old and with death, gently, in the spirit of the Day of the Dead. DearMeAndroidWrite letters to your future self; they stay sealed until the day you choose. GlowAndroidWrite down your wins, big and small, and get praised for them. Kinder to yourself. PrismAndroidChoose who you want to be in each part of life: partner, parent, colleague, friend. HoardAndroidA camera for moments worth keeping: three a day, kept as pixel-art treasure. The UsualAndroidA diary of the cafés you keep coming back to: each gets a page and a sugar packet, and every cup leaves a coffee stain on the page.

Open Lipovka Post in the morning and take today's bag. Then just go for your walk. Your steps carry the letters through the village, and at each gate someone tells you their news: a wedding, a lost sister found, a parcel with a live bee in it.

When you want a friend who isn't going anywhere

Visit your tree

Pick a real tree by your house or in the park and befriend it in Heartwood. Come when you can: hug it, listen to the bark, sit beside it with the phone face down. Every day you meet is a new ring in its cross-section, and if you vanish for a while, moss just grows on its card.

How they're made

Peace of mind shouldn't be a subscription.

Nothing leaves your phone

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your worries, journal, people, habits and birthday stay on the device. Anyway, Ties, Unhook, Dayquest, Serene and Ember don't even ask for internet access.

Free, with the source open

Every app is on GitHub. Download the APK or EXE directly, read the code, build it yourself. No trial that turns into a bill.

Made to be closed

No feed and no infinite scroll. Open for a minute, get what you came for, put the phone down. That's the whole point.
