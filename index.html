Anyway

Memento mori, minus the skulls

We're all gonna die anyway. So go do the thing.

You don't have years. You have Saturdays, about 4,000 of them if you're lucky. Anyway shows you that number, then helps you stop spending them on things the Sun won't remember.

Download APK Explore Anyway No internet permission at all
  • NowHow many Saturdays you have left, a question of the day, and the big orange ANYWAY button for the thing you're scared to do.
  • Worry FilterWill it matter in 5 years? In 100? When the Sun becomes a red giant? If not, it shrinks, spins and floats away.
  • Before it's too lateThings you'd regret not doing. Each one counts how long it has waited and turns orange after a week.
  • You're still aliveOne notification a day, at a random moment between 10:00 and 21:00.

SwissZen

The mindfulness multi-tool

Open the knife. A minute is enough.

Most mindfulness apps want a commitment. SwissZen is a pocket knife: unfold the one tool that fits the moment, use it, fold it back.

Download APK Explore SwissZen MIT licensed
  • BreatheA sphere that grows on the inhale. Box 4‑4‑4‑4, Calm 4‑7‑8, Coherent 5‑5, for 2, 5 or 10 minutes.
  • Wim HofGuided power-breathing rounds in four levels, with breath-hold targets from 0:30 up to 3:00+.
  • BellA mindfulness bell that rings with the app closed: random, by interval or at set times, with quiet hours.
  • PracticesFive micro-practices of 1–5 minutes and five meditations of 5–20.
  • JournalOne line a day, and an Echo: something you wrote weeks ago, brought back.

FocusShield

Deep work for Windows

Block the noise. Keep yourself honest.

A focus timer built on friction. While a session runs, distracting sites stop loading, chosen apps close, and quitting early means waiting 10 seconds and typing a phrase. When it ends, everything unlocks and you get a real break.

Download for Windows Explore FocusShield Windows 10 / 11 · 100% local
  • Intention firstEvery session starts with what exactly you'll get done, and ends with a check-in: did you make it?
  • Real blockingSites, YouTube tabs (even through a VPN), apps and folders stay locked until the timer ends.
  • Breaks that restore4‑7‑8 breathing with an animated circle, the 20‑20‑20 eye rule, a stretch and a glass of water.
  • A sprout that growsAn optional mascot grows a leaf with each pomodoro and blooms on the long break.
